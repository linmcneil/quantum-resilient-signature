\begin{abstract}
Quantum computers threaten classical public-key cryptography (RSA/ECDSA); NIST has
therefore standardized lattice-based primitives (ML-DSA/FIPS~204, FN-DSA/FIPS~206).
We report a reproducible, teaching-level study of two lightweight lattice-based
constructions aimed at blockchain transaction authentication: (i)~a symmetric LWE
authentication tag whose matrix is derived per transaction from the transaction hash
(``one-transaction-one-matrix''), and (ii)~a public-key lattice signature in the
Lyubashevsky Fiat--Shamir-with-aborts style.  Our contributions are methodological
and empirical: (a)~a parameter-instantiation workflow combining a root-Hermite /
core-SVP estimator with scaled-down LLL embedding attacks, validated by matching
``phase-transition'' experiments; (b)~a transaction-derived matrix variant that
removes the need to transmit/reuse matrix seeds; (c)~a benchmark suite aligned with
recent critiques of ML-DSA evaluation methodology, reporting full latency
distributions and rejection-sampling worst cases rather than means only.  All code,
data, and figures are open source and fully reproducible.  We do \emph{not} claim
formal security proofs; formal analysis and module/ring-structured instantiations
are left as future work.
\end{abstract}

\section{Constructions and Threat Models}
\label{sec:constr}
Track A（LWE 认证标签，MAC 语义）：$A=\text{Expand}(\mathrm{SHA256}(tx))$，
$b=A s+e \bmod q$，$s\in\{-1,0,1\}^n$，验签 $\|b-As\|_\infty\le V$。
Track B（公开可验证签名）：$pk=(A,T=A S)$，$S$ 短三元矩阵；签名
$z=y+S c$，$c=H(tx\parallel w)$、$w=A y$，拒绝采样直到 $\|z\|_\infty\le \kappa-h$；
验签 $H(tx\parallel A z - T c)=c$ 且 $z$ 短。
语义边界：Track A 不是公钥签名；伪造 Track B 等价于找短 $S'$ 满足
$A S'=T$（非齐次 SIS）。

\section{Transaction-derived Matrix (I2)}
\label{sec:i2}
$A=\text{Expand}(\mathrm{SHA256}(tx))$：免传矩阵种子、杜绝跨交易结构化复用；
安全性归约到带大量样本的搜索 LWE。边界：消息相关 $A$ 使对手可“挑”矩阵，
需要额外的归约论证（未来工作）。

\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:disc}
尺寸/安全/吞吐三维权衡；ringless 公钥 ~224 KB 说明 module/ring 结构的必要性
（ML-DSA pk 仅 1312 B）；教学级估计的局限（目标 $<$ GH 界时退化为维度上界）；
不构成形式化证明。
